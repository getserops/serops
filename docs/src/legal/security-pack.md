# Security pack

Three documents for your security and procurement teams. They come with
every Business license, and you can read them before you buy.

| Document | What it is | Get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Security overview** | Two pages: architecture, data flow, what leaves your network, masking, access control, audit, license-key checking, how to report a vulnerability. | [Read the overview](../security/overview.md) (your browser's print to PDF keeps the layout) |
| **Data-processing terms** | Standard terms for the only personal data SerOps the company can receive: order and invoicing contacts, and what you send to support. | [Read the terms](https://serops.ai/docs/legal/data-processing-terms/) |
| **Pre-filled security questionnaire** | Answers grouped by the domains of the CSA Cloud Controls Matrix v4, each with the document that supports it. | [Read it](security-questionnaire.md) or [download the spreadsheet (CSV)](../assets/security-pack/serops-security-questionnaire.csv) |

## Standard means as it is

Accepting the three documents as they are counts as signing our standard
terms: you stay on **Business**. Asking for changes to any of them, or for
your own questionnaire to be filled in, is **Enterprise**
([pricing](https://serops.ai/pricing)). Filling in your supplier
registration (company, bank and tax details, vendor portal) is included in
Business: it is administration, not a change to the terms.
