# SerOps data-processing terms

Version 1.0, 2026-11-22. Part of the
[security pack](https://serops.ai/docs/legal/security-pack/) and of the
[Enterprise Terms](enterprise-terms.md), which they complete. Terms defined
in Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") have the same meaning here.

## 1. What personal data SerOps can receive

SerOps is self-hosted and its license key is checked offline. SerOps the
company has no access to your Installations and receives no data from them:
no telemetry, no usage statistics, no error reports. The only personal data
it can receive are:

| Data | How it reaches SerOps | SerOps' role |
|---|---|---|
| Names, work email addresses and phone numbers of the people who order, sign, receive invoices or administer the license | The order, the invoice, the contact form, email | Independent controller, under the [privacy policy](https://serops.ai/privacy) |
| Whatever you choose to send for support or with a security report: messages, and attachments such as logs or screenshots, which may contain your staff's or your users' personal data ("Support Data") | Email to support@serops.ai or security@serops.ai | **Processor**, on your behalf, under these terms |

The license key holds the licensee's organisation name, not personal data.

These terms apply to Support Data. Contact data for the order and the
invoice is processed under the privacy policy.

## 2. SerOps' obligations as processor

SerOps:

1. processes Support Data only to answer the support request or security
   report it came with, which, with these terms, are your documented
   instructions, unless the law requires otherwise, in which case it tells
   you first where the law allows;
2. ensures that anyone authorised to process Support Data is bound by
   confidentiality;
3. applies the measures in section 7;
4. assists you, as far as reasonable, with data subject requests and with
   your obligations under GDPR articles 32 to 36;
5. tells you if it believes an instruction infringes data protection law.

## 3. What you do

Send only what the request needs. Remove special categories of personal
data, and personal data the request does not need, before you send
anything: SerOps' own masking runs inside your Installation, not on what you
email.

## 4. Sub-processors

You authorise the providers SerOps uses to receive and store support email.
SerOps gives you their names and locations on request, and gives 30 days'
notice before adding or replacing one, so that you can object on reasonable
grounds. If the objection cannot be resolved, you may stop sending Support
Data or end the affected license with a pro-rata refund. SerOps binds each
sub-processor to equivalent obligations and remains liable for it.

Your AI provider and the integrations you configure are your own
processors, not SerOps' sub-processors.

## 5. Personal data breach

SerOps notifies you without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming
aware of a breach affecting Support Data, with the information it then has.

## 6. Deletion

SerOps deletes Support Data when the request is closed and no later than 30
days after, and earlier on your request, unless the law requires it to be
kept.

## 7. Security measures

- Access to Support Data is limited to the people handling the request.
- Support Data is kept only in the mailbox that received it and in the
  places the request needs, and deleted as section 6 states.
- Email is encrypted in transit where the sending and receiving servers
  support it.

## 8. Audit

SerOps makes available the information needed to show compliance with these
terms, and allows audits on 30 days' written notice, during Business Hours,
no more than once a year unless a breach or a supervisory authority requires
it. Each party bears its own costs.

## 9. International transfers

SerOps is established in Tunisia. Where Support Data is transferred from the
European Economic Area to SerOps, the Standard Contractual Clauses of
Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module Two (controller to
processor), apply and are incorporated by reference, with section 1 of these
terms as the description of the processing and section 7 as the security
measures. If they conflict with these terms, the Clauses prevail.

## 10. General

Liability under these terms falls within the limits of the Enterprise Terms,
to the extent the law allows. These terms last as long as SerOps holds
Support Data. Contact: **privacy@serops.ai**.
